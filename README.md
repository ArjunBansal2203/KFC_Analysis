# 🍗 KFC Sales Analysis

## 📌 Introduction

Analyzed 8,000 KFC sales records across 5 countries from 2018–2024 to understand sales trends, customer behavior, and the impact of marketing spend.

## 🛠️ Technologies

Python · Pandas · Plotly · Streamlit · Statistical Analysis

## ✨ Features

- Interactive sales dashboard
- Country, year, month and branch filters
- Customer and marketing-spend analysis
- Sales trend and market comparison
- Statistical analysis of sales drivers

## 🔄 Process

1. Cleaned and prepared the sales data using Pandas
2. Analyzed yearly and market-level sales trends
3. Compared customer numbers and marketing spend with sales
4. Created interactive visualizations using Plotly
5. Built and deployed the dashboard with Streamlit

## 📊 Results

- **$174.9M** sales in 2024
- **6.04%** sales growth in 2024
- **78.9%** of sales variation explained by customer numbers + marketing spend
- **15.8%** improvement in sales per customer across marketing-spend groups
- Canada was the fastest-growing market at **3.84%**
